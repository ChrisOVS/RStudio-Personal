Your data

Everything stays on this device. Nothing you type is ever sent anywhere.

A restore link has your figures packed into it. The part after the # never reaches any server — browsers don't send it — but treat the link itself like the data: bookmark it or mail it to yourself, don't post it.

Why is there no Google or Apple sign-in?

Because this app has no server. It is one file that runs entirely in your browser, and it never sends anything anywhere — which is rather the point when the contents are your salary.

A real sign-in needs a backend holding secrets, and it would mean uploading your figures to it. The three options above cover the same ground without that: this device remembers you automatically, a backup file survives a wiped browser, and a restore link opens your figures on any other device.
